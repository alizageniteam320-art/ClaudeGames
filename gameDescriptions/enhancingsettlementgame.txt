Settlement Overseer: Pixel Kingdom A Living, Breathing Survival Strategy Engine
________________


Overview
Settlement Overseer: Pixel Kingdom is an immersive, real-time settlement management simulator that blends the charm of retro pixel art with the complexity of modern survival strategy. As the Overseer, you are tasked with transforming a lone shack into a thriving, fortified citadel. Your success hinges on your ability to balance resource loops, protect your citizens from nightly raids, and manage the intricate personal lives of your "Sims."
________________


Core Gameplay Mechanics
1. Dynamic Resource Ecosystem
Every material in the Kingdom serves a vital purpose. Unlike static strategy games, resources in Pixel Kingdom must be physically gathered and transported:
* Harvesting: Assign Sims to chop timber 🪵, mine granite 🪨, or extract iron ore ⚙️.
* The Food Chain: Hunt wild goats 🥩 or forage for herbs 🌿. These raw ingredients must be processed in a Kitchen to create nourishing food 🍖 that prevents starvation.
* Preservation & Crafting: Unlock the Preservation Room to access advanced chemistry. Convert herbs into Salt or Sugar to create high-tier dishes that provide health and energy buffs.
2. Sim Management & "The Human Element"
Your settlers aren't just numbers—they are individuals with unique traits and needs:
* Vitals: Track Hunger, Energy, and Happiness. A hungry Sim is a slow Sim; an exhausted Sim will eventually collapse.
* Life Cycles: Manage demographics through recruitment or the "Try for Baby" mechanic. Watch children grow from small, low-capacity helpers into full-sized workers.
* Skill Progression: As Sims perform tasks, they grow more efficient in specific trades (Woodcutting, Mining, Cooking, Combat).
3. Strategic Architecture
Build your town using a modular room system. ________________


Threats & Defense
The wilderness is not empty. As your settlement grows, it attracts the attention of Monsters.
* The Raid Timer: A persistent countdown keeps you on edge. You must balance your workforce between gathering resources and preparing for the next assault.
* Combat: Assign your strongest Sims to Guard or Fight tasks. Use your Town Security rating to mitigate losses during the chaos of a raid.
________________


Abstract Visuals & Atmosphere
Experience a "Lush Pixel" aesthetic featuring:
* Dynamic Environments: Watch clouds drift over your settlement while Sims perform animated tasks like swinging axes and carrying crates.
* Status Bubbles: Instantly read your settlement's health through visual cues—Sims will "think" of food or sleep when their needs are critical.
* Responsive UI: A high-tech glass-morphism interface that provides deep-dive stats while staying out of the way of the action.
Overseer's Pro-Tip: Don't expand too fast! Each new Sim is another mouth to feed. Ensure your Kitchen and Preservation rooms are running efficiently before recruiting more help.
