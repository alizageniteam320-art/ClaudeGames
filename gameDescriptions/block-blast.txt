________________


Master the Mechanics
The gameplay is deceptively simple but offers infinite depth. Your goal is to navigate an $8 \times 8$ digital arena using a revolving set of geometric shapes.
* Tactical Drag-and-Drop: You are dealt three unique pieces at a time. Each move requires careful calculation; one wrong placement could block your path to a new high score.
* The "Blast" Effect: Fill a horizontal row or vertical column to trigger a "Blast." Unlike traditional falling-block games, you have the freedom to clear lines in any direction, allowing for complex "cross-clears."
* High-Stakes Survival: There is no timer—only your own spatial limits. The game ends the moment you can no longer fit your remaining pieces on the board.
